Penal Code - PEN

PART 1. OF CRIMES AND PUNISHMENTS [25 - 680.4]

  ( Part 1 enacted 1872. )
  

TITLE 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE [92 - 186.36]

  ( Title 7 enacted 1872. )
  

CHAPTER 8. Conspiracy [182 - 185.5]
  ( Chapter 8 enacted 1872. )

  
183.  

No conspiracies, other than those enumerated in the preceding section, are punishable criminally.

(Enacted 1872.)
