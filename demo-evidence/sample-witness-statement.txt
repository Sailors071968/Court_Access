I, David Park, residing at 1840 Elm Street, provide the following voluntary statement regarding the incident at 1842 Elm Street on the evening of March 15, 2025.

At approximately 10:30 PM, I was in my living room watching television when I began hearing raised voices coming from next door at 1842 Elm Street. The walls between our units are thin and I could hear a man and a woman arguing loudly. I could not make out specific words at first.

At approximately 10:40 PM, the arguing became louder. I heard what sounded like something hitting a wall — a thud. Then I heard a woman's voice say "Stop" or "Don't" — I'm not 100% certain of the exact words but it was clearly a woman raising her voice in distress.

At approximately 10:43 PM, I heard what sounded like glass breaking. After the glass broke, there was a brief period of quiet, maybe 30 seconds, then the arguing resumed but at a lower volume.

I decided to call 911 at approximately 10:47 PM. I reported hearing arguing and breaking glass. The dispatcher asked if I heard any threats and I said I wasn't sure but the woman sounded scared.

I saw the police arrive at approximately 10:50 PM from my window. I observed one officer approach the front door of 1842 Elm. The door appeared to be partially open.

I did not witness any physical contact between the occupants. I can only report what I heard through the shared wall.

I have lived at 1840 Elm Street for three years. I have heard the couple at 1842 argue on previous occasions, maybe 4-5 times in the past year, but I have never heard breaking glass or thuds before this incident.

This statement is true and correct to the best of my knowledge.
