Vehicle Code - VEH

General Provisions

  ( General Provisions enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3. )
  
9.  

Whenever any notice, report, statement, or record is required by this code, it shall be made in writing in the English language.

(Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
