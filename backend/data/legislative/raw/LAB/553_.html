Labor Code - LAB

DIVISION 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION [200 - 2699.8]

  ( Division 2 enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90. )
  

PART 2. WORKING HOURS [500 - 890]

  ( Part 2 enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90. )
  

CHAPTER 1. General [500 - 558.1]
  ( Chapter 1 enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90. )

  
553.  

Any person who violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
