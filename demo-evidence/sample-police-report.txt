SUPPLEMENTAL POLICE REPORT
Case Number: 2025-CR-04821
Date of Incident: March 15, 2025
Reporting Officer: Officer J. Martinez, Badge #4471
Reviewing Supervisor: Sergeant K. Pham

NARRATIVE:

On March 15, 2025, at approximately 2247 hours, I, Officer J. Martinez, was dispatched to 1842 Elm Street regarding a report of a disturbance. Dispatch advised that a neighbor reported loud arguing and possible sounds of breaking glass.

I arrived on scene at approximately 2253 hours and observed the front door of the residence was slightly ajar. I approached the residence and announced my presence by stating "Police Department" in a loud, clear voice.

At approximately 2254 hours, the suspect, later identified as Michael Torres (DOB: 06/12/1988), opened the door. I observed that the suspect had what appeared to be a fresh laceration on his right hand. The suspect stated "I cut myself on a glass" when asked about the injury.

I observed the interior of the residence from the doorway. I noticed broken glass on the kitchen floor and an overturned chair in the living room. The victim, identified as Sarah Torres (DOB: 09/03/1990), was seated on the couch. The victim appeared to have redness on her left cheek and was visibly upset.

At approximately 2256 hours, I requested the suspect step outside to speak with me. The suspect complied and walked outside. I conducted a pat-down search of the suspect for officer safety. No weapons were found.

I asked the suspect what happened. The suspect stated that he and his wife were arguing about finances. He stated that he accidentally knocked over a glass from the counter, which broke on the floor. He denied any physical contact with the victim.

At approximately 2301 hours, Officer R. Chen (Badge #5523) arrived on scene to assist. Officer Chen spoke with the victim inside the residence while I remained with the suspect.

The victim stated to Officer Chen that the suspect grabbed her by the arm and pushed her against the wall during the argument. The victim stated the suspect then swept items off the kitchen counter in anger, which caused a glass to break on the floor. The victim stated that the suspect's hand was cut when he struck the counter.

At approximately 2315 hours, I placed the suspect under arrest for domestic battery (PC 243(e)(1)). The suspect was handcuffed and transported to Central Station for booking.

Photographs were taken of the victim's injuries (redness on left cheek, bruising on left upper arm) and the scene (broken glass, overturned chair).

Evidence collected:
- Photographs of victim injuries (6 photos)
- Photographs of scene (8 photos)
- Broken glass fragments (preserved in evidence bag)

END OF REPORT
