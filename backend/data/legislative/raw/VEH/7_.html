Vehicle Code - VEH

General Provisions

  ( General Provisions enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3. )
  
7.  

Division, chapter, and article headings do not in any manner affect the scope, meaning, or intent of the provisions of this code.

(Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1996.)
