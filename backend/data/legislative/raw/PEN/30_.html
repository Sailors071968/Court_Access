Penal Code - PEN

PART 1. OF CRIMES AND PUNISHMENTS [25 - 680.4]

  ( Part 1 enacted 1872. )
  

TITLE 2. OF PARTIES TO CRIME [30 - 33]

  ( Title 2 enacted 1872. )
  
30.  

The parties to crimes are classified as:

1. Principals; and,

2. Accessories.

(Enacted 1872.)
