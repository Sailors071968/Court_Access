Penal Code - PEN

PART 5. PEACE OFFICERS' MEMORIAL [15001 - 15003]

  ( Part 5 added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1518, Sec. 1. )
  
15003.  

Peace officer memorial ceremonies, including the dedication of the memorial and any subsequent ceremonies, shall be conducted by the California Peace Officers’ Memorial Foundation, Inc.

(Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 242. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
